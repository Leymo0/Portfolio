Roll for
the enemy

Pick a role, roll three champions, hand one to the player you're drafting for. Safe and spicy picks all work in that lane. Chaos picks do not — that's the whole point of them — and everyone gets a mandatory first legendary item on top. A mixed roll never serves more than one spicy or chaos card, so there's always a way out.

Running this as a full 2-team draft

This tool rolls for one lane at a time — to draft a full 5v5 with it, both teams just take turns using it manually, role by role. Go in role order: Top → Jungle → Mid → ADC → Support. For each role, that lane's two players pick for each other on this tool — you roll a champion for your opponent in the same role, and they roll one for you. Who rolls first alternates as you move down the role order, so it stays fair over a full draft:

  • TopTeam 1's top laner rolls first — for Team 2's top laner. Then Team 2's top laner rolls for Team 1's top laner.
  • JungleTurn to roll first flips: Team 2's jungler rolls for Team 1's jungler, then Team 1's jungler rolls for Team 2's.
  • Mid → ADC → SupportKeep alternating who rolls first, role by role, all the way down. Whoever didn't open the last role opens this one.
